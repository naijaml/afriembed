# Noman masara (Growing maize)

Ana shuka masara a farkon damina bayan ruwan sama ya fara sauka akai-akai. A yi amfani da iri mai inganci kuma a bar
tazara ta santimita 75 tsakanin layuka. Takin zamani na NPK ana zubawa makonni biyu bayan shuka, sannan urea bayan
makonni biyar. Farashin masara a kasuwa yakan tashi a lokacin rani, don haka adana masara a rumbu mai bushewa yana
taimakawa manoma su samu riba.
