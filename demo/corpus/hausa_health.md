# Rigakafin zazzabin cizon sauro (Malaria prevention)

Zazzabin cizon sauro cuta ce da sauro mai dauke da kwayar cutar plasmodium ke yadawa. Hanyoyin kariya sun hada da
kwana a karkashin gidan sauro mai maganin kashe kwari, da share wuraren da ruwa ke taruwa, da kuma shan maganin
rigakafi ga mata masu juna biyu. Alamomin cutar sun hada da zazzabi, sanyi, ciwon kai, da amai. Idan an ga wadannan
alamomi, a je asibiti nan take domin gwaji da magani.
